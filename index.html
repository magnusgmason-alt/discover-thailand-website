About the guide

One kingdom.
Every corner.

Thailand runs for more than 1,500 km from the hills of the Golden Triangle to the islands near Malaysia. Discover Thailand is a complete travel guide to the country: when to go, how to get around, where to stay, what to eat, what it costs, how to behave at temples, and which coast to choose in each season.

Limestone & sea

Islands carved from ancient reefs.

The towers of Phang Nga Bay, Krabi and Phi Phi are limestone that formed as reefs and sea-floor sediment millions of years ago. Rain and waves have since worn them into sheer cliffs, sea caves and hidden lagoons. On the other side of the peninsula, the Gulf islands of Samui, Phangan and Tao are granite, with rounded boulders and palm-lined bays.

A kingdom of many capitals.

Bangkok after dark

When the city comes alive.

Bangkok is at its best once the heat goes out of the day. Street food stalls fill the pavements, the riverside temples are lit up, and rooftop bars look out over one of the biggest cities in Asia. A few evenings spent eating your way through the night markets is part of any first trip.

The companion editions

Two coasts, three seasons.

From November to February the weather is at its coolest and driest across most of the country. March to May is the hot season. From May to October the monsoon brings rain to the Andaman coast, while the Gulf islands have their wettest months from October to December. Each companion book shows how to make the most of that.
